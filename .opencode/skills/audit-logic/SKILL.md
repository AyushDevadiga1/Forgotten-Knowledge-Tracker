---
name: audit-logic
description: Adversarial procedure for finding logic and invariant violations.
---

For the assigned scope, trace preconditions, state transitions, branch exclusivity, postconditions, and error paths.

Challenge boundary conditions: empty, null, zero, negative, minimum, maximum, duplicate, repeated, and unexpected-order inputs as applicable.

For each suspected defect, identify the violated invariant and a concrete proof path. Keep speculation separate from evidence.
