# P-007 — Documented enum contract bypassed by hardcoded literal writer

- PATTERN_ID: P-007
- ROOT_CAUSE_PATTERN:
  `concept_encounters.source` documents an enum
  (db/models.py:261: `''ocr'' | ''browser_extension'' | ''manual''`), but the only
  construction site of ConceptEncounter hardcodes `source="ocr"`
  (learning/concept_scheduler.py:127) regardless of the actual capture origin.

  and no consumer can distinguish capture pipelines.
